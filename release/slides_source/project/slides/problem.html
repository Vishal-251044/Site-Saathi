THE PROBLEM

Site workers learn by watching — mistakes are learned the same way

No training in their language

Most material is in English and written for engineers, not for masons and helpers on the site.

No internet, no time

Weak signal at the work area and short breaks. Training must be quick and work offline.

Safety is learned too late

Unsafe habits repeat when nobody explains the right method at the moment of work.

Keep this short. These are the problem statements from the PRD. Real numbers from the site visit come on the next slide.
