Title
Concrete Work – Basic Procedure
Description
How to mix, place and compact concrete so it becomes strong. Follow the engineer's mix and these steps.
Objective
Mix, place and compact concrete correctly and start curing on time.
Materials and tools
Cement Sand Aggregate (khadi) Water Measuring box Concrete mixer Needle vibrator or tamping rod Head pans Trowel and float Curing water or wet gunny bags
Safety precautions
Wear gloves, boots, a helmet and eye protection. Wet cement burns skin. Never put a hand or tool into a running mixer. Keep the vibrator cable dry and away from water. Do not stand under a loaded bucket or near the edge of the slab. Wash cement off your skin with clean water at once.
Common mistakes
Adding extra water Mixing by guess, not by measure Placing concrete late Dropping concrete from a height Poor compaction (honeycombs) Stopping curing early
