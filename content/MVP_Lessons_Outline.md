## SS-MAS-01 · Brick Masonry – Basic Procedure

**Objective:** Lay a straight, level brick course with full, even joints.
**Materials and tools:** bricks, cement, sand, water, trowel (थापी), plumb bob (ओळंबा), spirit level, string line, head pan (घमेले), measuring box.

**Main steps (what I already know):**
1. Soak the bricks in water before use so they do not suck water out of the mortar. *(soaking time — confirm)*
2. Mix mortar (मसाला) in the ratio the engineer gives *(e.g. 1:6 cement : sand for walls — confirm)*; use it before it starts to set. *(time limit — confirm)*
3. Mark the wall line and tie a string line at the course height.
4. Lay the first course on a full mortar bed; check it is level with the spirit level.
5. Lay each brick frog up, press it into place, fill every joint fully; keep joints about 10 mm *(confirm)*; break the vertical joints (no joint directly above another).
6. Check plumb (ओळंबा) and level every few courses and correct at once.
7. Clean and rake the joints; keep the brickwork wet (curing) for the days the engineer says. *(days — confirm)*

**Safety (red box):** helmet, gloves and safety shoes; do not throw bricks up to a person; work from proper scaffolding, not from loose brick stacks.
**Common mistakes (yellow box):** dry bricks · thick or empty joints · vertical joints in one line · no plumb check · no curing.

## SS-CON-01 · Concrete Work – Basic Procedure

**Objective:** Mix, place and compact concrete correctly and start curing on time.
**Materials and tools:** cement, sand, aggregate (खडी), water, measuring box, mixer, vibrator / tamping rod, head pans, curing material.

**Main steps:**
1. Before starting, the supervisor checks formwork and steel (सळई) are ready and clean.
2. Measure cement, sand, aggregate and water by box / batch as the engineer says (grade and ratio given by the engineer — never by guess).
3. Mix in the mixer until the colour is even; add only the measured water. *(mixing time — confirm)*
4. Carry and place the concrete quickly; do not drop it from a big height. *(time limit and drop height — confirm)*
5. Compact with the vibrator or rod until air bubbles stop; do not over-vibrate.
6. Level and finish the top surface.
7. Start curing when the surface is hard enough and keep it wet for the days the engineer says. *(days — confirm)*

**Safety:** gloves and boots (wet cement burns skin), eye protection, helmet; never put a hand or tool in a running mixer; keep vibrator cables dry and away from water.
**Common mistakes:** extra water "to make it easy" · poor compaction (honeycombs) · placing late · stopping curing early.

## SS-SAF-01 · Construction Site Safety – Basic Precautions

**Objective:** Use basic safety equipment and habits every day on site.
**Materials and tools:** helmet, safety shoes, gloves, reflective jacket, safety harness, first-aid box.

**Main steps:**
1. Wear helmet, safety shoes, gloves and jacket before entering the work area.
2. Look at the work area first: open edges, openings, loose material, wet floors.
3. At height, work only from proper scaffolding or a fixed ladder; tie the harness to a strong point.
4. Do not touch loose wires or electrical boards; only the electrician repairs them.
5. Lift heavy loads with bent knees and a straight back; ask for help.
6. Keep walkways clear; remove nails and waste.
7. Tell the supervisor at once about any danger or injury; know where the first-aid box is.

**Safety:** this whole lesson. AI rules apply: for height, electrical work or injuries the answer is always "stop and ask your supervisor or engineer".
**Common mistakes:** helmet carried, not worn · harness worn but not tied · working near open edges without a barrier · hiding small injuries.
