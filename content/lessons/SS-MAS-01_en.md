## Materials and tools
Bricks · cement and sand (mortar) · water and a tub · trowel · plumb bob · spirit level · string line and pegs · measuring box · head pan

## Steps (one per screen, with picture)
| # | Step text (1–2 short sentences) | Image |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Soak the bricks in clean water until no more air bubbles come out. Wet bricks do not suck water out of the mortar. | `SS-MAS-01_s1.jpg` |
| 2 | Mix mortar in the ratio your engineer gives, for example 1 cement to 6 sand. Measure with a box, and use the mortar within 30 minutes. | `SS-MAS-01_s2.jpg` |
| 3 | Mark the wall line. Fix a peg at each end and tie a tight string line at the height of the first brick. | `SS-MAS-01_s3.jpg` |
| 4 | Spread a full bed of mortar and lay the first row along the string. Check the row with the spirit level. | `SS-MAS-01_s4.jpg` |
| 5 | Lay each brick with the frog facing up and fill every joint fully, about 10 mm thick. Never put one vertical joint above another. | `SS-MAS-01_s5.jpg` |
| 6 | Every few rows, check the wall with the plumb bob and the spirit level. Correct a brick at once, while the mortar is soft. | `SS-MAS-01_s6.jpg` |
| 7 | Clean the joints at the end of the day. Keep the wall wet for 7 days (curing). | `SS-MAS-01_s7.jpg` |

## Safety precautions (red box)
- Wear a helmet, gloves and safety shoes.
- Do not throw bricks up to another person. Pass them by hand or in a head pan.
- Work from strong scaffolding, never from a stack of bricks.
- Wet cement hurts skin and eyes. Wash with clean water at once.
- Keep broken bricks and waste away from where you stand.

## Common mistakes (yellow box)
- Using dry bricks
- Thick or half-empty joints
- Vertical joints in one line
- Not checking plumb and level
- Mixing mortar by eye, or using old mortar
- Building too high in one day
- Stopping curing early

### Explanations (shown after each answer)
- **Q1:** Dry bricks pull water out of the mortar. The mortar becomes weak and does not stick well.
- **Q2:** Joints of about 10 mm are strong and even. Thick joints shrink and crack.
- **Q3:** A plumb bob (ओळंबा) hangs straight down, so you can see if the wall leans.
- **Q4:** When joints are staggered, each brick spreads the load to two bricks below. Joints in one line make a weak line that can crack.
- **Q6:** Water lets the cement in the mortar gain strength. Keep the wall wet for about 7 days.
