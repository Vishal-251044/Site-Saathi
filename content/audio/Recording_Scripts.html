SS-CON-01_en.mp3 English · SS-CON-01 · ~2.6 min (246 words)

  1. Concrete Work – Basic Procedure.
  2. Mix, place and compact concrete correctly and start curing on time.
  3. Step 1. Before you start, the supervisor checks the formwork and steel. The formwork must be strong, clean and without gaps.
  4. Step 2. Measure cement, sand, aggregate and water with boxes, as the engineer says. Never mix by guess.
  5. Step 3. Put aggregate, sand and cement in the mixer, then add the measured water slowly. Mix for about 2 minutes, until the colour is even.
  6. Step 4. Never add extra water to make the concrete easy to work. Extra water makes the concrete weak.
  7. Step 5. Carry and place the concrete quickly, within about 30 minutes of mixing. Do not drop it from a big height.
  8. Step 6. Compact with the vibrator until air bubbles stop coming up. Do not vibrate too long in one place.
  9. Step 7. Level the top surface. Start curing when the surface is hard, and keep it wet for at least 7 days.
  10. Safety precautions. Wear gloves, boots, a helmet and eye protection. Wet cement burns skin. Never put a hand or tool into a running mixer. Keep the vibrator cable dry and away from water. Do not stand under a loaded bucket or near the edge of the slab. Wash cement off your skin with clean water at once.
  11. Common mistakes: Adding extra water, Mixing by guess, not by measure, Placing concrete late, Dropping concrete from a height, Poor compaction (honeycombs), Stopping curing early.

SS-MAS-01_en.mp3 English · SS-MAS-01 · ~3.0 min (281 words)

  1. Brick Masonry – Basic Procedure.
  2. Lay a straight, level brick wall with full, even joints.
  3. Step 1. Soak the bricks in clean water until no more air bubbles come out. Wet bricks do not suck water out of the mortar.
  4. Step 2. Mix mortar in the ratio your engineer gives, for example 1 cement to 6 sand. Measure with a box, and use the mortar within 30 minutes.
  5. Step 3. Mark the wall line. Fix a peg at each end and tie a tight string line at the height of the first brick.
  6. Step 4. Spread a full bed of mortar and lay the first row along the string. Check the row with the spirit level.
  7. Step 5. Lay each brick with the frog facing up and fill every joint fully, about 10 mm thick. Never put one vertical joint above another.
  8. Step 6. Every few rows, check the wall with the plumb bob and the spirit level. Correct a brick at once, while the mortar is soft.
  9. Step 7. Clean the joints at the end of the day. Keep the wall wet for 7 days (curing).
  10. Safety precautions. Wear a helmet, gloves and safety shoes. Do not throw bricks up to another person. Pass them by hand or in a head pan. Work from strong scaffolding, never from a stack of bricks. Wet cement hurts skin and eyes. Wash with clean water at once. Keep broken bricks and waste away from where you stand.
  11. Common mistakes: Using dry bricks, Thick or half-empty joints, Vertical joints in one line, Not checking plumb and level, Mixing mortar by eye, or using old mortar, Building too high in one day, Stopping curing early.

SS-SAF-01_en.mp3 English · SS-SAF-01 · ~2.2 min (213 words)

  1. Construction Site Safety – Basic Precautions.
  2. Use basic safety equipment and safe habits every day on site.
  3. Step 1. Before you enter the work area, wear your helmet, safety shoes, gloves and jacket. Tie the helmet strap.
  4. Step 2. Look at the work area first. Watch for open edges, holes, loose material and wet floors.
  5. Step 3. At height, work only from strong scaffolding or a fixed ladder. Tie your harness to a strong point.
  6. Step 4. Never touch loose wires or electric boards. Only the electrician repairs them.
  7. Step 5. Lift heavy loads with bent knees and a straight back. Ask for help with heavy loads.
  8. Step 6. Keep walkways clear. Remove nails, steel pieces and waste.
  9. Step 7. Tell the supervisor at once about any danger or injury. Know where the first-aid box is.
  10. Safety precautions. For work at height, electrical work or any injury: stop and ask your supervisor or engineer. Never remove a guard rail or a cover over a hole. Do not stand or work under a load being lifted. Do not run or play on site.
  11. Common mistakes: Carrying the helmet instead of wearing it, Wearing a harness but not tying it, Working near an open edge without a barrier, Hiding small injuries, Using a broken ladder.
