>
> I am working on **SITE SAATHI**, a free offline Android learning app for construction workers, for Civilathon (Construction Technologies). It gives short lessons with pictures and audio in Marathi, Hindi and English, opened by QR codes at work areas.
>
> I need your help with two things this week:

> 2. **A content reviewer.** Could you, or a site engineer you know, review 3 short lessons (Brick Masonry, Concrete Work, Site Safety) by **Wednesday 25 Nov**? Each lesson is about 6 steps with a 5-question quiz. Your name and qualification would appear in the app as reviewer.

> Vaibhav Chandrakant Jadhav · Civil Engineering · RIT, Sangli

## 2. Phone call script — site engineer

**English**
> Good [morning], Sir. I am Vaibhav Jadhav, civil engineering student at RIT Sangli. [Prof. Name] gave me your number.
> I am building a free learning app for construction workers: short lessons in Marathi, Hindi and English with pictures and audio.
> May I visit your site **tomorrow, Thursday 19 Nov**, for about two hours? I want to ask 5 workers and 1–2 supervisors a few short questions about their work, language and phones.
> I will not take names or phone numbers. Photos only of the work, only if you allow.
> What time is best, and should I bring a letter from the college?

**मराठी**
> नमस्कार सर. मी वैभव जाधव, RIT सांगलीचा सिव्हिल इंजिनिअरिंगचा विद्यार्थी. [प्रा. नाव] यांनी तुमचा नंबर दिला.
> मी बांधकाम कामगारांसाठी एक मोफत शिकण्याचे ॲप बनवत आहे — मराठी, हिंदी आणि इंग्रजीत चित्र आणि आवाजासह छोटे धडे.
> उद्या, गुरुवार १९ नोव्हेंबरला, सुमारे दोन तास तुमच्या साइटला भेट देऊ शकतो का? ५ कामगार आणि १–२ सुपरवायझरना कामाबद्दल काही छोटे प्रश्न विचारायचे आहेत.
> मी नावे किंवा फोन नंबर घेणार नाही. फोटो फक्त कामाचे, तुमची परवानगी असेल तरच.
> कोणती वेळ योग्य राहील? कॉलेजचे पत्र आणू का?

*(Marathi text is a draft — check wording with a native speaker if unsure.)*

**After the call, write down:** date and time agreed · who gave permission (role) · site area · whether a letter is needed · any rules (helmet, entry gate, photo limits).
